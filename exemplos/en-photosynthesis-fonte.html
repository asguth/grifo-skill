Key point

The light reactions turn light into chemical energy (ATP, NADPH); the Calvin cycle spends it to build sugar from CO₂.

Must know
Memorize
  • Chlorophyll absorbs mostly blue and red; reflects green.
  • Water splitting (photolysis) releases O₂.
  • Limiting factors: light, CO₂, temperature.
Common trap

✗ "The Calvin cycle only happens in the dark." → It does not need light directly, but depends on ATP/NADPH from the light reactions.

✗ "Plants photosynthesize instead of respiring." → Plants do both.

Tip

Follow the atoms: the C and O in glucose come from CO₂; the O₂ released comes from H₂O.
